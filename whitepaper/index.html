The problem
02 · The problem

Demand for video exploded.
Making it didn't get easier.

Brands post every day, on every platform, in every language their customers speak. Production hasn't kept up.

01

Too slow, too expensive

A single animated or VFX ad means scripting, casting, sets, shooting or animation, voice, edit and grade: weeks of work and a crew. Most small brands and creators simply can't afford it.

02

Ten tools for one video

Today's AI tools each do one step: an image model here, a video model there, a separate voice tool, a separate editor. Characters drift between shots, and stitching it together is still manual work.

03

Stuck at five seconds

Most AI video stops at short clips. Audiences, YouTube and broadcasters want stories: 5 to 10 minute episodes with the same characters, narration and chapters.

04

The language gap

Leading tools are English-first. Arabic, Hindi and regional-language audiences, hundreds of millions of viewers, are underserved, though they are where growth is.

Who feels it most

  • Brands & D2C sellers who need ads every week.
  • Creators & channels who need consistent characters and series.
  • Agencies & studios under pressure on cost and turnaround.
  • Media & education needing long-form explainers in local languages.

Technology
05 · Technology

An orchestration layer
over the best models.

Foundation models get better every quarter. Our value is the layer above them: turning an idea into a plan, keeping characters and sets consistent, routing every step to the right model, and assembling a finished film.

LayerWhat it does
Story & planningBreaks an idea or script into scenes, shots, characters, sets and dialogue; produces the shot list the rest of the pipeline follows.
Character LockTurns character sheets and reference images into a locked cast, so faces, outfits and proportions stay consistent across shots and episodes.
Model routerSends each task to the best engine for it (today ByteDance Seedance 2.5 / 2.0 for video, Seedream 5 for images and ElevenLabs-grade voices) and swaps engines as better ones ship.
Stories engineOur in-house 2D vector (SVG) animation and motion-graphics renderer for long-form episodes and reels: scenes, captions, chapter cards, mixing.
Cost enginePrices every render from the provider's real cost with a guaranteed gross margin, and shows the price before anything is generated.
Media pipelineStores every output permanently, streams long films (HLS), and delivers platform-ready masters, thumbnails and captions.

Model-agnostic

No lock-in to one AI vendor; quality rises as the models do, while our workflow, data and characters stay ours.

Safe by design

Spend locks on paid renders, permanent storage of outputs, and consent rules for real people's likenesses.

Built, not slideware

Studio, credits, billing, storage, the Stories engine and the live website are already built and running.

Business model
10 · Business model

Pay per render,
with margin built in.

Customers buy credits and spend them on any module. Every render is priced from the AI provider's real cost plus a fixed margin, so revenue scales with usage and every render is profitable.

Revenue streamWhoHow it's priced
Credit packsCreators, small brandsOne-off packs from US$29 (600 credits) to US$199 (5,400 credits); no subscription, credits don't expire
Team & agency plansAgencies, studiosMonthly seats plus pooled credits to confirm
Enterprise & APIMedia, education, platformsAnnual contracts, volume pricing, Arabic/long-form pipelines to confirm
Studio servicesBrands wanting done-for-youPer-project production via our studio team (as Blacklight Motion does today)
40%minimum gross margin on every generated render, enforced by the cost engine
$0.037per credit on the largest pack: the floor the margin is calculated on
0renders sold below cost: prices update when provider rates change

Example: a 15-second Pixar-style reel

Provider cost on Seedance 2.0 at 720p ≈ US$2.25 → customer price 86 credits ≈ US$3.17. The same reel from a traditional animation studio typically costs hundreds to thousands of dollars and takes days.
